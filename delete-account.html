Account & data control

Delete your MeToYou account

Use this page to request deletion of your MeToYou account and the personal information associated with it—even if you no longer have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Use the button below to create an email request addressed to the MeToYou developer.
  2. Send the request from the email address linked to your MeToYou account so that we can verify ownership. We may ask for limited additional verification before deleting an account.
  3. We will process verified requests within 30 days and confirm the outcome by email.

Request account deletion →

If the button does not open an email app, write to noorimahdi031@gmail.com with the subject “MeToYou Account Deletion Request”.
Security: Do not email passwords, OTP verification codes, bank details, or device authentication tokens.

What the request covers

After identity verification, the request covers your account profile, authentication records, device association and premium entitlement data managed on our servers, except for records that must be retained for a legitimate legal, security or dispute-resolution purpose. We will explain any such retention when applicable.

Accounting records and credit-transfer history stored only on your phone are not accessible to us and cannot be remotely erased by the deletion request. You can remove them from your own device by clearing app data or uninstalling MeToYou. Exported backup files must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

Deleting an account does not automatically delete messages you independently sent through WhatsApp or Telegram; those services have their own data controls.
