01 About MeToYou

MeToYou (میتیو) is an Android app for initiating mobile credit transfers and other operator USSD actions in Afghanistan, and for recording transactions, customer balances, receipts and reports. It also provides optional premium features.

This Privacy Policy applies to the MeToYou app and its related authentication and subscription services. MeToYou is developed and operated by Mahdi Noori.

Our privacy contact is noorimahdi031@gmail.com. We aim to keep the records you enter on your own device where possible.

Important: Sending a USSD request involves your mobile network operator. The operator—not MeToYou—processes the network request and may apply operator charges and its own privacy terms.

02 Information you choose to provide

Account and authentication

When you sign in using an email verification code (OTP), we process your email address. If you choose Google sign-in, we receive account details made available with your consent, such as your name and email address. You may also provide your name or business name as profile information.

Premium activation and support

We process your account's premium-plan status, plan name, activation and expiry information. If you choose the manual activation/contact flow, you can provide your name and telephone number in a message you decide to send through WhatsApp or Telegram. Those messages are handled by the messaging service you choose.

Transactions and customer records

Phone numbers used for credit transfers, customers' contact details you choose to save, transaction history, amounts, debts, receipts and reports are stored in the app on your device as described below. They are not automatically uploaded to our account server as part of ordinary local record-keeping.

03 Device, usage and diagnostic information

For authentication and device-bound premium access, our account server may receive a device identifier based on Android ID (or a locally generated identifier), device model, Android version, app version and your sign-in information.

When applicable, advertising, analytics or notification SDKs may process advertising identifiers, app interaction events, diagnostic information, and notification/device tokens. The precise information depends on the services enabled in your version and their SDK configurations.

Crash and diagnostic logs may be retained locally. If remote crash reporting is configured and enabled in a particular release, relevant diagnostic information may be transmitted to the configured service.

04 Phone permissions and USSD requests

MeToYou uses Android permissions in connection with the features you select:

  • Call phone: to initiate a supported USSD/calling action for credit transfers after you use the relevant app function.
  • Phone state: to support telephony-dependent features and detect necessary phone/SIM state.
  • Contacts: to help you select a saved contact or number when you use that function. We do not describe this as an automatic upload of your entire address book.
  • Internet and network state: for authentication, subscription verification, ads and optional online services.
  • Notifications: to display notifications when permitted by your Android settings.

When you initiate a credit-transfer USSD action, the target phone number, transaction amount and the relevant operator code may be passed through Android's phone interface to your mobile network operator to process your request.

Permission transparency: Android permissions can depend on the installed app version. The final Google Play Data safety declaration should be checked against the actual release build and included third-party SDKs—not inferred from this page alone.

06 How we use information

  • To create accounts, verify sign-ins, and keep your account secure.
  • To enable the USSD actions and local accounting functions you request.
  • To validate premium access and provide related support.
  • To display ads in the free experience and understand app performance where enabled.
  • To deliver notifications and diagnose errors where applicable.
  • To comply with legitimate legal requirements and address misuse or disputes.

We do not sell your personal account information. Information may be processed by service providers as explained in the next section.

07 Third parties and sharing

Depending on the options you use and services included in your installed build, MeToYou may interact with:

  • Google Sign-In: optional sign-in and sharing of selected Google account details after consent. Google privacy policy.
  • Google AdMob: in-app advertising and ad measurement. How Google uses information.
  • Firebase Analytics: app usage analytics. Firebase privacy information.
  • OneSignal: push notification delivery and associated device/notification data. OneSignal privacy policy.
  • WhatsApp and Telegram: only if you choose to contact us or send a manual activation message through one of those apps. WhatsApp policy; Telegram policy.
  • Mobile network operators: network and USSD processing for transactions you initiate, under the operator's terms and practices.
  • MeToYou's account and subscription server: authentication, associated device details and premium entitlements.

We may disclose limited information when required by applicable law or to respond to valid legal requests. Your use of external services is also subject to their own policies.

08 Data stored on your phone and backups

Transaction records, customer information, debts, local balances and reports are kept in the app's local storage. This information is not part of the regular authentication or subscription data sent to our server.

When you choose to create or share a backup, the file is saved or transmitted to the location or app you select. Keep exported backups secure and remove unwanted copies yourself. Uninstalling the app usually removes its local app data, but it does not remove files that you exported to other locations.

The account session and premium state may also be cached locally for app functionality.

09 Security practices

Our configured production authentication and subscription API uses HTTPS. The app attempts to protect locally stored session credentials with Android encrypted storage, with an app-private preferences fallback on devices where encrypted storage cannot be initialized. Optional device or biometric locking can help protect app access.

We work to reduce unnecessary data transfers, but no system is completely secure. You should protect your device, verify recipients before transferring credit, and safeguard exported backups.

10 Retention, your rights and account deletion

Account profile, session/device association and premium status information are retained while needed to operate your account, or for legitimate security, support, legal or dispute-handling reasons.

You can request deletion of your MeToYou account and server-side personal data by emailing us from the address connected with your account. We will verify ownership and aim to process verified requests within 30 days. We may need to retain limited information where legally required; we will explain that when applicable.

How to delete your account →

You can also delete app data stored on your own phone. Removing server-side account data does not automatically erase exported backup files or messages that you independently sent through third-party messaging services.

11 Advertising and device controls

The free app may display ads served by Google AdMob. Where available, your device's ad settings and Google privacy controls allow you to manage the advertising identifier and ad personalization. Some ads or measurements may still be delivered without personalized targeting.

Learn more about how Google uses data through Google's partner sites notice.

12 Children's privacy and international processing

MeToYou is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum applicable age in their country), and we do not knowingly seek personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided account data, contact us to request its removal.

Some service providers may process information in countries outside Afghanistan. Such processing is governed by applicable law and each provider's privacy practices.

13 Changes and contact

We may revise this policy when the app, third-party integrations or legal requirements change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes may also be announced through the app or website where practical.

For privacy questions, requests to review your account data, or account deletion, contact noorimahdi031@gmail.com.
