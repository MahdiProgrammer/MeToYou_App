1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the MeToYou mobile application (the “App”, package name noori.mahdi.metoyou) collects, uses, shares, and protects your information. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

We designed MeToYou to collect only the minimum information needed to provide its features. The App is an accounting and financial-management tool; most of the financial information you enter stays on your own device.

3 Data we collect

3.1 Account & sign-in information

To create your account and sign you in, we collect:

  • Email address — entered by you for the email verification-code (OTP) login, or provided by Google when you choose Sign in with Google.
  • Name — your name and (optionally) a business name associated with your account. When you sign in with Google, your Google profile name may be used.

3.2 Device information

When you sign in, the App sends the following to our authentication server so we can secure your session and bind it to your device:

  • Device identifier — a stable per-install identifier derived from the Android ANDROID_ID (or a randomly generated value as a fallback).
  • Device model (manufacturer and model name).
  • Operating-system version (Android version and SDK level).
  • App version.

3.3 Subscription & purchase status

If you activate a premium subscription, we store and process your subscription status, plan name, and expiry date so the App can unlock premium features and verify your access.

3.4 Analytics, diagnostics & push notifications

Through the third-party services listed in section 6, we and our providers automatically collect:

  • Usage & interaction data (e.g., screens opened, events, approximate session activity).
  • Diagnostic & crash data (e.g., crash logs and performance information).
  • Identifiers such as the Advertising ID and push-notification tokens.

3.5 Financial records you enter

Transactions, balances, customers, products, and other accounting data you enter are treated as your content. As described in section 7, this content is stored locally on your device.

5 How we use your data

  • To create and authenticate your account and keep you signed in securely.
  • To provide and operate the App's features, including premium features tied to your subscription.
  • To secure your account, bind your session to your device, and help prevent fraud and unauthorized access.
  • To understand and improve the App through aggregated analytics and crash diagnostics.
  • To send notifications related to the App where applicable.
  • To show advertising that helps keep the App free (see section 8).
We do not sell your personal information.

6 Sharing & third-party services

We share data only with the service providers that make the App work. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Sign-In (Google LLC) — authentication. When you tap “Sign in with Google”, Google shares your name, email address, and profile picture with the App after your consent.  Policy
  • Google AdMob (Google LLC) — in-app advertising. Collects the Advertising ID and interaction data to serve and measure ads.  Ad policy · How Google uses data
  • Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) — usage analytics and diagnostics.  Policy
  • OneSignal — push-notification delivery; processes a notification token and basic device information.  Policy
  • Our authentication & subscription server — operated for the App to manage logins and premium access; receives the data described in sections 3.1–3.3.

We may also disclose information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of users or the public.

7 Data stored on your device

The financial and accounting records you create in the App are stored locally on your device. The authentication token that keeps you signed in is stored locally using Android's encrypted storage.

If you use the App's backup feature, a backup file is created only when you choose to do so and is saved to the location you select (for example, your device storage). You are responsible for the security of any backup files you export or share.

Uninstalling the App removes locally stored data; backup files you have exported are not removed automatically.

8 Advertising & Advertising ID

The App may display ads through Google AdMob. AdMob uses the device Advertising ID and interaction data to serve and measure advertising. You can control this:

  • On your device, open Settings → Google → Ads to reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization.
  • Learn more about how Google uses data from apps that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

9 Data security

  • All communication between the App and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Your local authentication token is stored using encrypted storage on the device.
  • Optional biometric / device lock can be enabled to protect access to the App.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

10 Data retention

We keep account data (such as your email, name, device identifier, and subscription status) for as long as your account remains active or as needed to provide the App. When you request deletion (section 11), we delete or anonymize your account data, except where we must retain certain records to comply with legal obligations or resolve disputes.
