Privacy Policy

Meteo — Credit Transfer via USSD

This Privacy Policy explains how the Meteo app (“the App”) handles information. The App helps users dial USSD codes for mobile credit transfer and keeps a local history for convenience.

Last updated: 2026-02-26 Package: noori.mahdi.metoyou Applies to: Android

Quick summary

We do not run user accounts History is stored locally on your device Ads may use device identifiers

The App may show ads using Google AdMob. Ad providers may collect and share device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID) and other information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Important: Even if the App’s main features work without sending your data to our servers, third-party SDKs (such as advertising SDKs) can transmit data off the device.

1) Information we collect

The App is designed to work primarily on-device. However, depending on features you use and the SDKs included, some data may be processed.

Category Examples Where it is stored Shared off-device?
Local transaction history Phone numbers entered for credit transfer, timestamps, notes (if any) On your device (local storage) No (not sent to our servers)
Device or other IDs Advertising ID / app instance identifiers (used by ads/measurement) Processed by advertising SDKs Yes (by third parties such as AdMob)
Diagnostics & performance Basic diagnostics, crash/performance signals (if collected by SDKs) Processed by third-party services (if enabled) May be shared by those services
Note: The App does not ask you to create an account, and we do not maintain a user database on our servers.

2) How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

  • App functionality: to show your local history and help you repeat a previous USSD transfer quickly.
  • Analytics & measurement: to understand app performance and ad measurement (handled by advertising SDKs, where applicable).
  • Fraud prevention & security: advertising SDKs may use signals (including identifiers) to prevent fraud and abuse.

3) Sharing & third parties

We do not sell your personal information. The App may include third-party SDKs (for example, advertising) that collect and share certain data off-device as described below.

  • Local history data (including phone numbers you enter) is intended to stay on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • Advertising/measurement data may be collected/shared by third parties (e.g., AdMob) for ads, measurement, and fraud prevention.

Third-party services have their own privacy practices. Please review their policies.

4) Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App may show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and share data such as: device identifiers (including the Advertising ID), IP address, ad interaction data, and diagnostics to deliver, measure, and improve advertising and to prevent fraud.

Useful links:
• Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
• How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Your controls: You can reset or limit ad personalization from Android settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording may vary by Android version).

5) Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by the App. Local history stays on your device. Data transmitted by third-party SDKs is generally sent using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. If you suspect a security issue, please contact us.

6) Data retention & deletion

Local history: You can delete the App’s local data by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the App. Since we do not operate user accounts or a server-side database for your history, we generally cannot delete data stored only on your device for you.

  • Delete local history: Settings → Apps → Meteo → Storage → Clear data
  • Or uninstall the App to remove local files created by the App (Android behavior may vary).
Reminder: Advertising-related data handled by third parties (such as AdMob) is governed by their policies and controls.

7) Children’s privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child provided information to the App, please remove the App and clear its data on the device.

8) Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.

If you continue using the App after changes become effective, you agree to the updated policy.
